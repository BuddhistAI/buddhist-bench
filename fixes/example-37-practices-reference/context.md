The Thirty-Seven Practices of Bodhisattvas (rgyal sras lag len so bdun ma) is a short verse text by the Tibetan master Gyalsé Tokmé Zangpo (1295–1369). It sets out thirty-seven practices of a bodhisattva, followed by concluding verses.
