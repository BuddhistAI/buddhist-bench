# Nak Tham (Thai national Dhamma exams)

Imported exam track: the Thai national Dhamma exams for monks and novices (Nak Tham) and lay people (Dhamma Studies), 2020, 2022 and 2023, three levels each (elementary, intermediate, advanced), in Thai. See [SOURCE.md](./SOURCE.md) for origin, license and changes.

- **Status:** importer ready (`scripts/import_nak_tham.py`); data not yet imported.
- **Grading:** multiple choice, keyed from the official answers as published by the source.
- **Reviews:** imported items keep the exam office's answer keys and are not individually reviewed. A Thai reviewer should spot-check a sample, add topic tags, and file disputes for doubtful keys.
- **Contamination:** published online, so marked `public`. Newer papers obtained directly from the exam office would be added as held-out papers.
