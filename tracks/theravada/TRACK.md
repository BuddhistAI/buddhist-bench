# Theravāda track

Questions on Theravāda doctrine, Abhidhamma, Vinaya, commentarial tradition, history and practice, judged by Theravāda reviewers. Sub-traditions (Sri Lankan, Burmese, Thai, Thai Forest, Lao and Cambodian, modern vipassanā movements) are tagged with their IDs from `taxonomy/traditions.json` so results can be reported for each.

Good sources: the Pali canon and commentaries (with PTS or SuttaCentral references), the Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha, national monastic exams. The Thai Nak Tham exam questions published by Mahidol University's biodatlab are Apache-2.0 and could seed a Thai-language set (keep their attribution and license note in `sources_license`).

Languages to aim for: `en`, `th`, `my`, `si`, `km`, `lo`, `pi`.

Owner: placeholder until a Theravāda team takes the track (see `.github/CODEOWNERS`).
