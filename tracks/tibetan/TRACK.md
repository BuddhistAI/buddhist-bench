# Tibetan track

Questions on the Tibetan and Himalayan schools (Nyingma, Kagyu, Sakya, Gelug, Jonang, Rimé, plus Mongolian and Bhutanese Buddhism), judged by reviewers from each school. Where schools differ, items either name the school (`attributed`) or list each school's position (`contested`, `default-probe`). Do not key a school's own view as the Tibetan view.

Good sources: Kangyur and Tengyur (Tōhoku numbers), BDRC records, monastic curricula and debate manuals of each school.

Languages to aim for: `en`, `bo`, `zh`, `mn`, `dz`.

Owner: placeholder until reviewers from each school join (see `.github/CODEOWNERS`).
